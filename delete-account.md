# Delete your Solia account

You can delete your Solia account and its data at any time.

## In the app

Open **Profile → Delete account** and complete the identity-verification steps. This is the fastest route.

## By email

Email **privacy@getsolia.com** from the address associated with your account. We may request additional identity verification and respond within the period required by applicable law.

## What deletion does

- Deletes the authentication record and account-owned profile, schedule, fitness, nutrition, social, score, coaching-report, and storage data.
- Deletes the complete one-to-one message thread, pair background, and mute state for both participants when either participant deletes their account.
- Removes the departing account's membership, content, and identifiers from surviving groups and businesses. Ownership transfers deterministically when another member remains; empty containers may be deleted.
- Removes moderation reports filed by the departing account. A report filed by another user may survive with its target account ID removed and replaced by a random deletion-specific correlation key.
- May leave another user's match-history record without a live link to the deleted profile.

## Delayed cleanup

Deleted messaging media, pair backgrounds, and group avatars or backgrounds can leave a restricted service-only cleanup item containing a storage path and retry metadata. Cleanup waits at least 24 hours and deletes only after current reference checks find no live product reference. An Apple-linked deletion can leave a restricted revocation item until Apple confirms token revocation. These records are not available to app users and are retained only until the work succeeds.

## Backups

Deleted data may remain in access-restricted backups for up to about 30 days before those backups expire.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy) for the complete retention and deletion terms.
