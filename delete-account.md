# Delete your Solia account

You can delete your Solia account and its data at any time — you do not need to reinstall the app to make the request.

## In the app (fastest)

**Profile → Delete account.** This permanently deletes your data across our database.

## By email

Email **privacy@getsolia.com** from the email address associated with your account, asking us to delete it. We respond within the time your local law requires (30 days in most places).

## What gets deleted

Your profile, schedule, fitness and nutrition data, photos, social graph, reports, scores, and your authentication record.

Match-history rows that belong to *other* users (a past opponent's record that they played someone) are retained on their side, without your profile.

## Backups

Deleted data may persist in encrypted database backups for a limited period (up to about 30 days) before those backups expire.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for full detail.
